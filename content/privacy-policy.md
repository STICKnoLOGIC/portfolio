# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction
**STICKnoLOGIC** ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (https://sticknologic.is-a.dev) or submit content.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Analytics Data (Umami)
We use **Umami**, a privacy-focused, self-hosted analytics tool. 
- **Data Collected:** We do not use cookies. Umami collects pseudonymous data such as page views, browser type, operating system, device type, and approximate location (country/city). 
- **IP Addresses:** Your IP address is anonymized (hashed) and never stored in plain text. 
- **Retention:** Analytics data is retained for **365 days** (default Umami setting) and then automatically purged. 
- **Legal Basis:** Legitimate Interest (to improve site performance and understand audience). 

### B. User Submissions (OpenForms)
When you submit a blog post or contact us via **OpenForms**:
- **Data Collected:** Name/Username, Email (optional/for contact), Social Links, Avatar URL, Short/Long Bio, and Blog Content.
- **Storage:** Upon approval, your content is converted to Markdown and stored in a **public GitHub repository**. Your personal data (name, bio, links) becomes part of this public record.
- **Deletion:** Raw form submission data is deleted from our server immediately after the content is processed and published. However, once published to GitHub, the data remains publicly accessible in the repository history unless explicitly removed via a commit. 

### C. Third-Party Links
Our site contains links to external platforms (e.g., **Buy Me a Coffee**, **Itch.io**, **PayPal**).  Clicking these links redirects you to their servers. We do not control their data practices. Please review their respective privacy policies. 

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- To publish and display your blog post and author profile.
- To analyze site traffic and improve user experience (Umami).
- To respond to your contact inquiries. 

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure
We do not sell your data. Data is shared only:
- Publicly on our website and GitHub repository (for submitted posts).
- With service providers necessary for operation (e.g., hosting provider in Montreal, Canada). 

## 5. Your Rights
Depending on your location (EU, Philippines, California), you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data.
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data. 
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data (note: this may require unpublishing your blog post from our GitHub repository).
- **Opt-Out:** Opt-out of analytics tracking (see below).

To exercise these rights, contact us at **contact@sticknologic.is-a.dev**.
